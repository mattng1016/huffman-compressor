Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel was an Indian freedom fighter and statesman. He was born on 31st October 1875 in Nadiad, Kheda district in Gujarat. He finished his education late but went on to become a successful lawyer. Under the influence and leadership of Mahatma Gandhi, he joined the country’s freedom struggle and actively took part in non-violent civil disobedience as well as quit India movements against the British Raj. He joined the Indian National Congress and headed their Gujarat division. Later he went on to become the 49th president of the Indian National Congress.
After India became independent, Sardar Patel became the first Home Minister and Deputy Prime Minister of India. He organised relief efforts for people fleeing to India from Pakistan during the partition of 1947. He worked hard to restore peace in the country. He had an iron will and believed in national unity. Sardar Patel persuaded 562 princely states in the country to join together to form the Republic of India. Due to his contributions in the fight for independence as well as in the political integration of the country, he was nicknamed the ‘iron man of India’. 
By the summer of 1950, Sardar Patel’s health started to decline rapidly. He died after suffering a massive cardiac arrest on 15th December 1950 in Bombay, aged 75. He will always be remembered as one of India’s greatest leaders.
